# The Last Projectionist: Visual Bible

Catalogue slot 03.01 | Companion to the treatment, scene outline and screenplay | Target form: graphic novel, with a screen-test animatic as an option

---

## 1. Visual thesis

The book draws two kinds of image and keeps them apart: **what exists in the building** and **what someone says happened**. The reader should always be able to tell which kind of page they are on, and never be able to tell, from the drawing alone, whether an account is true.

- **Observed material** is hard-edged, charcoal, heavy black, with the grain of paper and film. Objects have weight, scratches and handling marks.
- **Reconstructions** are drawn inside one shared marker (section 5). The marker says "provisional, attributed to a speaker." It never says "false" or "true."
- **The one exception** is the closing screening, where the screen itself is the image (section 8).

Everything else in this document serves that rule.

---

## 2. Palette

The book is near monochrome with a small number of controlled colours. Colour is information.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Booth Black | #1B1C1F | Shadows, booth interior, outlines on observed material |
| Paper | Leader White | #E9E3D4 | Page ground, labels, logbook, leader tails |
| Midtone | Cinder Grey | #6B6C70 | Charcoal wash, dust sheets, concrete |
| Projector light | Lamp Amber | #E7B04A | Beam, booth lamp, café window at dusk |
| Marker | Frame Silver | #9DB2B6 | Perforations and inner border of every reconstruction frame |
| Hot object | Van Red | #B3261E | The red van only (and the red of the exit signs, kept dimmer) |
| Pencil | Graphite | #3A3B3F | Annotations, handwriting, Priya's pad |

Rules:

- **Van Red appears in exactly three kinds of place:** inside reconstruction frames (as the van), on the actual screen in Scene 18, and as dim exit signs in the auditorium. It never appears on an observed object. This keeps the van a recurring mark of "someone's story" and lets Scene 18 land.
- **Frame Silver is used for the marker and nothing else.** No other element in the book uses it.
- **Lamp Amber** belongs to light sources only. It never tints a character's skin or clothing.
