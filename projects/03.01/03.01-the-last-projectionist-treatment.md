# The Last Projectionist: Treatment

Catalogue slot 03.01 | Cycle: Work and Its Afterlives | Framework: inverse problems, underdetermination | Scale M

## Logline

Nine days before a condemned cinema closes, its head projectionist finds handwritten corrections on old prints that seem to document a scene audiences remember but the films no longer contain. He builds a coherent account of what was removed and why, accuses the wrong man with it, and learns that the evidence he needed had been in his own booth the whole time.
