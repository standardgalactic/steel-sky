# The Last Projectionist: Scene Outline

Catalogue slot 03.01 | 18 scenes in three acts | Three locations: auditorium, projection booth, Pell's Café (cellar as a subsidiary space of the café)

## Governing rules

- **1987 appears only as attributed reconstruction.** Every image of the festival night is drawn from a named source (Aurel's drawing, Benedikt's telling, Tobias's account, Daniel's letter) and carries the shared reconstruction marker: perforation-edged frames with a flickering inner border. The marker never signals which account is right.
- **The one exception is Scene 18**, where the supplementary passage appears as the actual screen in the actual auditorium. It confirms what was projected. It is not an omniscient flashback to Daniel's departure.
- **Two separate inverse problems run in parallel.**
  - *Problem A, the screening history.* H1 cover-up cut at Harlan Voss's order. H2 conflated memory. H3 source censorship before delivery. H4 one-night insert. H5 physical loss.
  - *Problem B, Daniel's departure.* D1 left voluntarily with help. D2 harmed by someone connected to the Voss family. D3 left alone, circumstances unknown.
  - Aurel's central error is binding A and B into one explanation. His correction is to separate them and weigh the evidence for each independently.
- **Timing fact.** 41 feet of 35 mm at 24 fps runs about 27 seconds. The nine minutes is the festival's scheduled intermission, during which the insert was projected. The logbook records that the house lights were lowered again for a supplementary projection. The return to the feature is the second additional changeover.
- **Aurel's arrival.** Thirty-eight years of service puts him in the booth from about 1988, the season after the disputed festival. He inherited the logbook and has handled its pages for his whole career without reading that entry as evidence.
- **Structural device.** Priya's inventory count runs down with the deadline. Each scene opens on a day number and a count of items still unassigned (screened, transferred, retained, disposed). The count reaches zero in Scene 18.

What each observation does:

- O1 fits H1, H3, H4 and strains H2 and H5. It does not identify who "H.V." is.
- O2 fits H1, H4, H5. It does not date the memory or fix its source.
- O3 shows the delivered print lacked the footage, subject to the label's provenance. It eliminates H1 and H5 for the delivered print. It does not rule out a later insert and removal.
- O4 shows a separate projection and narrows toward H4. It does not say what was projected.
- O5 with O6 and O7 makes H4 strongly supported through conjunction. For Problem B, the letter plus two participants' accounts support D1. The screening records give chronology, not intention.

## Act One: An explanation takes shape

**Scene 01. The last inventory.** Booth, morning. Day 9.
Aurel prepares the projectors with practiced precision. Priya sets out the inventory procedure. He knows where everything belongs; she asks how he knows which version of each film an object represents. They disagree practically: should a repaired print be described only by its title? The deadline arrives through the work itself. Everything must be screened, transferred, retained or disposed of before the building is surrendered. Aurel treats the inventory as an intrusion on his last week.
*Live:* nothing yet. *Visual:* the count card, the booth in hard charcoal, no reconstruction marker anywhere.

**Scene 02. Two hands on the leader.** Booth, afternoon.
Rewinding *The Salt Orchard*, Aurel finds O1. Two other prints carry similar notes. He recognizes Edmund's hand but not the second. He reads "H.V." aloud as "Harlan Voss." Priya transcribes it exactly and leaves the expansion blank. First small difference between their methods.
*Live:* H1, H3, H4. *Visual:* the leader as a flat observed object, the two hands rendered distinguishably.

**Scene 03. The scene Benedikt remembers.** Café, evening.
Benedikt describes the red van, the lake-road turn, the cello phrase and a disturbance in the audience. He places it at the 1987 harvest festival unprompted. Aurel begins with open questions, then introduces the idea of a removal. Benedikt responds to the suggestion, but the scene preserves which details came before it. Margo recognizes the evening and steers talk to the closing programme.
*Live:* H1, H3, H4, H5. *Visual:* first reconstruction frames appear, attributed to Benedikt only.

**Scene 04. A missing passage.** Auditorium and booth, next morning. Day 8.
