## Conventions used on the page

- **TERMINAL HEADER.** Each scene opens on the register terminal's header: days until works begin, and the register's count of dwellings. It is an observed object, drawn hard-edged.
- **SECTIONS.** Building sections are drawn from one fixed viewpoint at one fixed scale. Each register has its own section. Slugs name which one is on the page.
- **No supernatural content.** Everything in the building can be measured. The strangeness is in the paper.
